Decision-Making with Data: Data removes guesswork and brings clarity.

Data-Driven vs Data-Inspired:
-> Data-Driven = only data-based (e.g., pricing after sales analysis).
-> Data-Inspired = mix of data + human judgment (e.g., new show ideas from watch trends).

Quantitative vs Qualitative Data:
-> Quantitative = numbers (e.g., 70% prefer fast delivery).
-> Qualitative = opinions/feelings (e.g., why customers trust fast delivery).


🔑 Together, both give the what + why behind decisions.
