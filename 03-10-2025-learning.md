-> Dashboards & Reports
- Enable analysts to visualize trends and track key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Tableau: Interactive, dynamic dashboards for deep insights.
- Spreadsheets: Flexible, detailed analysis with formulas and pivot tables.
- Helps stakeholders make informed decisions faster.

-> Data vs Metrics
- Data: Raw facts or figures collected from sources.
 Example: Daily sales numbers, website visits, sensor readings.
- Metrics: Quantitative measures derived from data to track performance.
 Example: Monthly revenue growth, conversion rates, average session duration.
-Understanding the difference ensures correct analysis and reporting.

-> Mathematical Approach to Problem Solving
- Applying formulas, statistics, and algorithms for structured analysis.
- Enables accurate insights, predictive modeling, and optimization.
Example: Using regression to forecast sales, or correlation to find relationships between variables.
